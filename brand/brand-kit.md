# Emdax brand kit

## Brand idea
Good tech. Great hands. Emdax brings practical care and clear thinking to the devices people rely on.

## Logo
Use the supplied Emdax Solutions logo in `assets/emdax-logo-gold.png` on light backgrounds. Preserve its proportions and leave clear space around it. For dark surfaces, use a light panel behind the logo rather than recolouring it. The supplied artwork is raster; request the original vector file for large print applications.

## Palette
- Espresso: `#302017`, headings and dark surfaces
- Bronze: `#80582F`, buttons and primary actions
- Gold: `#B98A44`, accents and focus states
- Ivory: `#FAF8F3`, primary background
- Sand: `#F1E9DD`, secondary background

Use espresso for text on light surfaces and white on bronze buttons. Reserve gold for larger accents rather than small text on white.

## Typography
- Manrope ExtraBold: headlines; close tracking and compact line height.
- DM Sans Regular, Medium and Bold: body copy, navigation and labels.

## Graphic language
Draw on the supplied logo's warm bronze and gold. Use clean layouts, practical service illustrations and photography of devices, tools and hands. Avoid busy effects that compete with the logo.

## Voice
Knowledgeable without jargon. Warm without being overly casual. Explain the problem, the next step and what the customer can expect. Never promise a repair, price or turnaround time before it has been assessed.
