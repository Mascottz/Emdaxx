# Emdax brand kit

## Brand idea
Good tech. Great hands. Emdax brings practical care and clear thinking to the devices people rely on.

## Logo
Use the supplied Emdaxx Solutions Ltd logo in `assets/emdax-logo-gold.png` on deep slate or charcoal backgrounds (`#1B2530` / `#141D26`) so both the metallic gold mark and brushed silver typography remain crisp and legible. Preserve its proportions and leave clear space around it. Do not stretch, recolour or crop out elements of the full lockup unless using the standalone gold emblem for small square icons (such as `assets/favicon-32.png` and `assets/apple-touch-icon.png`). The supplied artwork is raster; request the original vector file for large print applications.

## Palette
- Deep Slate: `#1B2530` / `#141D26`, header, footer, headings and dark surfaces
- Warm Gold: `#D4A743`, header call-to-action, accents and focus states
- Rich Gold: `#9E731C`, text highlights and icon accents on light surfaces
- Cool Paper: `#F6F7F9`, primary background
- Soft Mist: `#EAEEF4`, secondary surface background

Use deep slate for body headings and primary buttons on light surfaces, and dark slate text on warm gold call-to-action buttons for strong contrast.

## Typography
- Manrope ExtraBold: headlines; close tracking and compact line height.
- DM Sans Regular, Medium and Bold: body copy, navigation and labels.

## Graphic language
Draw on the supplied logo's deep slate, metallic gold and brushed silver. Use clean layouts, practical service illustrations and photography of devices, tools and hands. Avoid busy effects that compete with the logo.

## Voice
Knowledgeable without jargon. Warm without being overly casual. Explain the problem, the next step and what the customer can expect. Never promise a repair, price or turnaround time before it has been assessed.
