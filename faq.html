Helpful information about repairs, buying and selling computers, and visiting Emdax in Dutse.

GOOD TO KNOW

How can we help?

What computer problems can Emdax help with?

We handle laptop and desktop hardware issues, motherboard repairs, BIOS reprogramming, software problems and upgrades. Tell us your device model and the symptoms so we can discuss the next step.

How much will a repair cost?

Costs depend on the fault, the device and any parts needed. We can discuss pricing and options after assessing your device, before you decide whether to proceed.

How long will a repair take?

Timing depends on the issue and whether suitable parts are available. Ask us for an estimate after we have assessed your device.

What should I do before bringing in my computer?

Back up important files if you can, remove external drives and accessories, and tell us about any sensitive data. Do not send passwords in a public message. Ask us how device access will be handled if needed.

Do you offer a warranty?

Warranty terms can vary by service and part. Please ask about the terms for your device before approving any work.

Can I buy or sell a computer through Emdax?

Yes. We also buy scrap and non-working systems. Contact us for current availability and prices, or send the make, model and condition of the computer you would like to sell.

Where is Emdax located?

Find us at Olajumoke plaza, opposite Zenith Bank, First-gate, Dutse-Alhaji, Dutse, Bwari, FCT, Abuja. You can also message us on WhatsApp at +234 803 050 6052.
