5 simple ways to keep your laptop running smoothly

Your laptop does a lot of work in a small space. Heat, storage pressure and neglected updates can turn a reliable machine into one that feels frustratingly slow. These checks are a useful place to start before assuming you need a replacement.

1. Give it room to breathe

Use your laptop on a firm surface rather than a bed or cushion. Soft material can block the vents and trap heat. If the fan is constantly loud or the case feels unusually hot, shut the machine down and have the cooling system checked. Do not spray liquid into the vents.

2. Make space on the drive

A nearly full drive can affect updates and everyday performance. Review large downloads and files you no longer need, then move important documents to a backup drive or trusted cloud storage. Empty the recycle bin only after checking its contents.

3. Keep updates under control

Operating system and application updates can fix security problems and improve compatibility. Install them from trusted sources, keep the charger connected during major updates and restart when prompted. If an update repeatedly fails, make a note of the error rather than trying random fixes.

4. Protect your files before trouble starts

Keep at least one separate copy of documents, photos and work projects. Test that you can open the backup. A computer can fail without warning, and a backup stored only on the same laptop will not help if its drive stops working.

5. Pay attention to small changes

A loose charging port, flickering screen or battery that suddenly drains deserves attention. Stop using a swollen battery and disconnect the charger if it is safe to do so. Tell your technician when the problem started and what changed; those details can make diagnosis more useful.
